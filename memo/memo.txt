
RAY TRACING BOOK CH 1

Fundamental quantity of light along a ray: SI unit SPECTRAL RADIANCE, constant along ray (in vacuum). often only RADIANCE is used, unless you're making a SPECTRAL RENDERER

SHADOWS: hard shadows only have an UMBRA (part fully in shadow), but soft shadows have a
PENUMBRA (partly shadowed parts)

Kajiya-style or PATH TRACING: Light reflects off matte surfaces. A path starts at the camera and ends at the light. With proper care, this results in an unbiased (=matching phys. reality) result.

Importance sampling: Using a nonuniform sampling PDF.

Formally, renderers attempt to solve the transport equation, aka rendering equation for graphics-specific problem.

Ray marching: March along a ray, sampling along the direction. Used for volumetric surfaces. Hart's sphere tracing algorithm.
